# Aspectori competitor review — 18 October 2026

## Verdict and scope

Aspectori is not demonstrably the best product in the market. Its defensible direction is a clear, inspectable chart: uncertainty carried through the result, house-system differences made visible, and readings attached to their inputs. Free access is useful but common. Free PDF export is also offered elsewhere.

This review samples 12 relevant competitors across established web calculators, editorial sites and mobile products. It is not an exhaustive census or a hands-on benchmark of every paid feature. Findings describe first-party public pages retrieved through web search; search cache age varies. Prices and catalogue totals are not treated as durable comparisons. A blank or unmentioned feature means unverified, not absent.

## Product comparison after this change

| Capability | Aspectori status | Remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free natal chart, no account or email | Implemented | Measure completion and real user comprehension. |
| Published calculation checks | 22 reference charts; engine comparisons and documented historical offset exceptions | Broader dates, locations and boundary-case coverage; independent expert review. This is not proof that every chart is correct. |
| Unknown-time output | Day ranges, possible sign changes; no fabricated Rising or houses | Better explanation of approximate-time ranges and ambiguous historical records. |
| House comparison | Placidus / whole sign side by side, Porphyry reference, polar limitation | More systems only if user evidence supports them. |
| Free PDF | Direct browser-generated file, selectable text, wheel, settings, placements, reading, aspects and selected dated transits | Independent reader feedback; tagged PDF accessibility has not been established. |
| Synastry | All qualifying contacts retained by our method, plus three highlights | Biwheel, house overlays and composite are not implemented; no “complete synastry suite” claim. |
| Education | Four focused guides plus method and accuracy pages | Named qualified editorial review, original worked examples and a correction process. |
| Celebrity charts | Source-rated collection, searchable directory and crawlable category pagination | Most imported records have no birth time. More individually sourced timed charts and better filters would improve value. |
| Transit experience | Selected twelve-month transit windows | Not a full daily transit calendar, solar-return or progression calculator. |
| International reach | English interface | Validate actual country demand before translating; professional localization and regional search research required. |

## Practical priorities

1. Test the birth-entry → understandable result → PDF flow with new users. Record task completion, confusing terms and download failures, not just aesthetic preferences.
2. Have an experienced independent reviewer evaluate calculation explanations and a representative set of reports. Publish real credentials and corrections only when available.
3. Validate demand for a synastry biwheel and house overlays before building them; this is a visible feature gap in direct comparisons.
4. Improve selected high-value celebrity pages with source-specific context instead of multiplying template pages.
5. Keep the present PDF free during acquisition. If paying customers later justify an expanded paid report, add a clear paid offer, server-side entitlements and a separate free-versus-paid explanation. Do not silently turn the current download into a surprise checkout.

No performance, conversion, traffic or ranking superiority is established by this review. Those require production measurement, a defined comparison set and repeatable tests.
